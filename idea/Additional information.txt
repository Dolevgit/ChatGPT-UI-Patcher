I've attached a UI mockup illustrating the proposed behavior.

The mockup demonstrates:
1. A full-size main conversation with the regular history sidebar.
2. Multiple minimized temporary chat tabs floating at the bottom.
3. A small floating chat window opened by clicking a tab.
4. Separate controls for minimizing, expanding, saving, and closing temporary conversations.
5. Expanding a temporary chat into the main conversation area without saving it to history.
6. Returning to a regular conversation through the sidebar.

Temporary tabs should survive application restarts, but closing a tab should permanently remove access to that conversation, subject to the application's existing data retention policies.

Ideally, this feature could reuse existing Temporary Chat infrastructure while introducing a more convenient multitasking interface.

The attached mockup is a conceptual illustration, not a screenshot of an existing feature.

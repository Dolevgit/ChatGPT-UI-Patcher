I'd like to introduce **Floating Temporary Chat Windows** to the ChatGPT desktop app, allowing users to open multiple disposable conversations without interrupting their main conversation.

The idea is to combine a regular, full-size ChatGPT conversation with smaller, floating temporary chat windows that can be minimized into tabs at the bottom of the application.

### Proposed Behavior

**1. Main Conversation**
- The main conversation remains the primary workspace, displayed in the full-size chat area.
- Regular conversations continue to be saved in the existing conversation history in the sidebar.
- Users can return to any regular conversation through the sidebar at any time.

**2. Floating Temporary Chat Windows**
- Add a dedicated button with a flame icon to create a temporary conversation.
- Clicking a temporary chat tab opens a small, floating chat window above the main interface.
- The main conversation remains visible behind the floating window.
- Each temporary chat has its own independent conversation context.
- Multiple temporary conversations can exist simultaneously.

**3. Persistent Tabs at the Bottom**
- Minimized temporary conversations appear as compact, floating tabs at the bottom of the application.
- Clicking a tab reopens its small chat window.
- Tabs remain available until explicitly closed, including across application restarts.
- Temporary conversations do not appear in the regular conversation history.

**4. Temporary Window Controls**

Each floating window should include:

- **Minimize:** Hide the floating window while keeping its tab available.
- **Expand:** Display the temporary conversation in the full-size main chat area, without converting it into a regular conversation.
- **Save to History:** Convert the temporary conversation into a regular, permanent conversation and add it to the sidebar history.
- **Close:** Discard the temporary conversation and remove its tab.

Closing a temporary conversation should display a confirmation warning explaining that the conversation cannot be recovered.

**5. Expanding a Temporary Conversation**
- Expanding replaces the currently displayed main conversation with the temporary conversation.
- The temporary conversation remains temporary, and its tab stays available.
- The previously displayed regular conversation remains saved in history.
- Selecting any conversation from the sidebar returns the user to a regular conversation.
- The expanded temporary conversation can still be accessed through its bottom tab.

### Why This Would Be Useful

Not every question deserves a permanent conversation in chat history.

Users frequently need to ask quick questions, troubleshoot unrelated issues, explore ideas, or perform small tasks while working on something more important.

Currently, creating a separate regular conversation for every small question adds unnecessary clutter and interrupts the main workflow.

Floating temporary chats would allow users to:

- Keep their main conversation as their primary workspace.
- Ask unrelated questions in smaller windows without losing context.
- Keep temporary conversations open for minutes, hours, or even days.
- Quickly switch between multiple temporary conversations.
- Expand a temporary conversation when more space is needed.
- Save useful temporary conversations permanently when necessary.
- Discard conversations that are no longer relevant.

The key principle is simple:

**Regular conversations belong in history. Temporary conversations belong in floating tabs until the user explicitly saves or closes them.**
